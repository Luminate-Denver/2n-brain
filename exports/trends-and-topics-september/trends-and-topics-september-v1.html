From the Founders

August was another strong month for the 2^n community. We welcomed eleven family offices, added new Deal Partners, and reached a milestone with the first job posting live in Talent.

None of that happens on its own. It happens because members make introductions, share what they are seeing, and show up for one another. Thank you for that. That’s what makes this community work, and you keep proving it out.

On October 5, we are releasing features members have asked for since day one. Signal, Events, Messaging, and Radar all serve the same purpose: the more connected this network becomes, the more each of you gets out of it.
