## Sponsor Member Program — Driven By Your Referrals

No new sponsors were admitted in March — and that's by design. The 2^n Sponsor/GP Program is referral-driven and merit-based. We maintain a strict **5:1 family office to sponsor ratio**, so spots are limited and every admission has to be earned.

The strongest sponsor additions come from members who've worked / invested with them directly. If there's a fund manager, GP, investment bank, or independent sponsor you'd personally vouch for — someone who is best-in-class and would genuinely add value — we want to hear about it.

How To Refer A Sponsor Member:
- Think of a manager, GP, or service provider you believe in.
- Send an email introduction to Matt or Sydney with a brief note on why they'd be a fit.
- We'll vet them and follow up. Your name stays attached — strong referrals are recognized in the Amplifier Program.
