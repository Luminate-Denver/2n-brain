02Sponsor Member Program

Driven by your referrals.

No new sponsors in March — by design. The 2^n Sponsor/GP Program is referral-driven and merit-based. We maintain a strict 5:1 family-office to sponsor ratio, so spots are limited and every admission has to be earned.

The strongest sponsor additions come from members who've worked with them directly. If there's a fund manager, GP, investment bank, or independent sponsor you'd personally vouch for, we want to hear about it.
