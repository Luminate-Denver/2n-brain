Trends & Topics — May 2026: 3 design directions

Same April content rendered three ways for comparison. Pick one (or mix) to proceed with composition.

Direction A
Editorial
Magazine-style. Cream + navy. Big serif headlines (Instrument Serif). Pull-quote stats. Generous whitespace. Reads like a Bloomberg Wealth quarterly.
Direction B
Private-Bank Dispatch
Bordered. Compressed. Roman numeral sections, dotted ledger rules. Closer to a boutique private-bank monthly. More structured + scannable.
Direction C
Modern Minimal
Contemporary fintech. Inter sans + Instrument Serif accents + JetBrains Mono labels. Hairlines, big numbers, mono section codes. Almost editorial-tech.
