From the Founders

March was a milestone month. We welcomed a record 14 new Family Offices — the 2^n community has now reached the Eastern hemisphere — celebrated the first Gold status in the Amplifier Program, and locked in the date for our inaugural Family Office Summit in Denver, June 10–11.

None of this happens without you. Every introduction, every deal shared, every conversation at a dinner — that's what makes this community compound. This month's brief focuses on two things: recognizing the people driving that growth, and making it easy for you to keep it going.

II.Sponsor Member Program

Driven by your referrals.

No new sponsors were admitted in March — and that's by design. The 2^n Sponsor/GP Program is referral-driven and merit-based. We maintain a strict 5:1 family-office to sponsor ratio, so spots are limited and every admission has to be earned.

The strongest sponsor additions come from members who've worked with them directly. If there's a fund manager, GP, investment bank, or independent sponsor you'd personally vouch for — someone best-in-class — we want to hear about it.
