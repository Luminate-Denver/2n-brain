From the Founders

The month of May reminded me why we built 2^n. We welcomed nine new family offices this month, each one arriving through a member who thought of us, and to everyone who made an introduction, thank you. The members adding the most value here aren't always the loudest in the room; they're the ones quietly opening a door for a peer they respect, and we notice.

We also got out on the road, where we do our best listening. New York gave us a week of good conversations and a great 2^n Dinner on the 21st. You could feel the camaraderie the second the room filled, and seeing those connections continue on the platform afterward is exactly what we knew this would become.

So keep introducing the family offices and deal partners who belong here; every right person you bring makes the whole community stronger. Speaking of which, the 2^n Talent Network is up next. Family offices will be able to post roles and lean on the network to refer people they trust. If you have a thought on how we can serve this community better, send it my way.

Deals are the catalyst. In 2^n, a co-investment opportunity isn't just a transaction. It's the fastest, most natural reason for two families to get on a call and compare notes. That's one way relationships form here.

It's also how every family in this room effectively expands their deal flow: when you co-invest alongside another member, you're leveraging their network, their diligence, their conviction, and they're leveraging yours.

Use the + button to post a co-invest you'd like to share; 2^n reviews every deal before it goes live. And use the action buttons. They matter. A thumbs up on a deal isn't just a like; it's a signal. Tap it and 2^n will facilitate a call with the family or deal partner who posted it. The more you engage, the better the marketplace gets at surfacing the kinds of deals this room actually wants to see.

Access to deals you won't find through traditional channels is one of the most valuable parts of being in 2^n.

Engagement is what unlocks it.
